Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

Overview

Captain H ("we," "us") provides free community fitness and nutrition consultations in Al Ain, UAE. This page explains what information this website collects and how it is used.

What this website does not do

This website does not run a server-side database and does not store the details you type into the contact form. The "Continue to WhatsApp" button simply builds a pre-filled WhatsApp message from what you typed and opens WhatsApp in a new tab — your details are sent directly to Captain H's WhatsApp number, the same as if you typed the message yourself.

Information shared via WhatsApp

Any name, phone number, goal, or message you choose to send via WhatsApp is received directly in Captain H's WhatsApp inbox, subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy. We use this information only to respond to your consultation request and do not sell or share it with third parties.

Analytics and cookies

If analytics or advertising tracking (such as Google Analytics or the Meta Pixel) is enabled on this site, it is used only to understand how visitors use the site and to measure the effectiveness of any community outreach. Non-essential tracking is only activated where required with your consent.

Third-party links

This site links to Instagram and WhatsApp. Once you leave this site, those platforms' own privacy policies apply.
