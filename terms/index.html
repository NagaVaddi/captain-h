Captain H is a free, volunteer community service offering fitness and nutrition consultations and community events, including the 30-Day Walking Challenge, in Al Ain, UAE. It is not a commercial personal training business, and no fees are charged for consultations.

Fitness and nutrition guidance provided by Captain H is educational and motivational in nature. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified physician before starting a new exercise or nutrition program, particularly if you have an existing medical condition, injury, or are pregnant.

Participation is voluntary and at your own risk

Physical activity carries inherent risk. By participating in consultations, the 30-Day Walking Challenge, or any related activity, you acknowledge that you do so voluntarily and at your own risk, and that you are responsible for assessing your own fitness to participate.

Individual results from any fitness or nutrition guidance vary and are not guaranteed. Advice is based on the information you share and general best practice, not a personalized medical assessment.
